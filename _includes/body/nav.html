{%- comment -%} Overrides the theme's `body/nav.html` (in the jekyll-theme-hydejack gem) to render `site.menu` as a collapsible tree instead of a flat list. A menu entry with a `submenu:` list gets a caret button next to its link. The caret toggles the subpages; the label itself stays a normal link to the section page. Expanding is handled by the script in `_includes/my-body.html`, which also opens the section matching the current URL and marks the current page. The
